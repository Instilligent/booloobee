# Booloobee — Advertising & ASO Package

Positioning: **silly, shareable, 30-second “wait what?” farm game** — rainbows, unicorns, glitter pipeline. Not a hardcore sim.

## App Store metadata (draft)

**Name:** Booloobee Ranch  
**Subtitle:** Scoop. Sparkle. Sell.  
**Keywords:** unicorn,farm,ranch,idle,casual,3d,glitter,funny,kids,rainbow,supply,chain  

**Promotional text:**  
Turn pink unicorn glitter into boxed treasure. Scoop, spa, grind, box, sell — hire robots, blast pests with rainbows. Easy to pick up, hard to put down.

**Description:**
```
Welcome to Booloobee Ranch — the silliest supply chain on the App Store.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Scoop pink sparkle piles in the field
2. Wash them in the bubble spa
3. Grind pure glitter
4. Box with bows
5. Sell to the queue

Upgrade every station right where it lives. Hire farmers, spa crew, sellers, and robots. Collect stars, heal at the fountain, tip the jukebox. When pests show up — unicorns and dinosaurs — hose them with rainbows.

FEATURES
• 18 story stages across 5 acts
• 5 playable characters
• Real-time supply chain you can automate
• Area upgrades (buy at the station they affect)
• Soft, friendly difficulty — built for short sessions

Made for landscape play on iPhone. One more crate…
```

## Social / paid creative angles

1. **Shock humor:** “I made a game about unicorn poop and people kept playing.”
2. **Loop clarity:** 5-step icons, no text overload.
3. **Kid-safe silly:** family-friendly crude, not mean.
4. **ASO:** target “unicorn game”, “funny farm game”, “casual 3d”.
